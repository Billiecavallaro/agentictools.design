Questions

Sponsoring, answered

What does a Featured spot look like?

A card at the top of one use-case page, above the recommended setup, labeled Featured. It shows your tool's name, one sentence you write (up to 140 characters) and a link to your site.

Does sponsoring change the recommendations?

No. The setups, alternatives and directory listings are chosen on their own. A sponsor can't buy a place in them, and buying a spot doesn't remove a competitor from the page.

Which use cases are open?

All 14 have one Featured spot each, sold a month at a time, first come first served. The list on this page shows which are open.

How do I book?

Email agentictools.design@gmail.com with your tool, the use case or issue you want, and your start date. We'll confirm it fits and send an invoice.
