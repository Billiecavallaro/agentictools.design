What is agentictools.design?

agentictools.design is a free directory of AI agents and AI tools for product designers and product managers, updated daily. Each listing explains what the tool does in about 30 seconds, who it is for, what it is best at and what it costs, and is checked against the product's own website. It lists 27 agents across 6 categories, plus 6 workflows that chain them together.

What are the best AI agents for product designers?

These are the design agents in the directory right now, newest first:

  • Figma agent by Figma: An AI agent inside Figma Design that plans and carries out multi-step design tasks on your canvas.
  • Wonder by Aquila Labs: A design canvas where you and your coding agents work on the real product, and the design stays as code.
  • Figma Make by Figma: Turns prompts, frames and design kits into working, editable prototypes inside Figma.
  • Stitch by Google Labs: Generates mobile and web UI screens plus front-end code from text or sketches.
  • Uizard by Uizard: AI UI design tool that generates screens, themes and prototypes from prompts.

What is an AI agent workflow for design and product work?

A workflow is a short, repeatable chain of AI tools that ends in something you can ship or share. For example, Library-true screen to shipped code uses Figma agent, Wonder, Claude Code:

  • Draft the screen with the Figma agent using your own components
  • Bring it into Wonder and explore variations on the canvas
  • Have Claude Code build the chosen version in its own worktree

The result: A reviewed preview built from your design system, ready to merge. The directory has 6 workflows.
