Run user interviews at scale with an AI moderator

Outset writes the interview guide from your goals and runs hundreds of AI-moderated interviews at once. Pick Listen Labs instead when you need many languages. Either way, keep the results in Dovetail so themes add up over time.

Best for: Research teams and PMs who need many conversations, fast.

Watch out: An AI moderator scales your guide. If the guide asks leading questions, you get leading answers at scale, so pilot it on two or three people first.

  1. OutsetRuns the interviews
  2. DovetailStores cited insights

How it works

  1. Outset: Generates the guide from your research goals, runs video, voice or text interviews in parallel, then delivers themes and highlight reels. See site (demo)
  2. Dovetail: Holds the results next to the rest of your customer feedback, with every insight citing its source. Paid; from $4,800/yr plus credits; 30-day free trial

Or swap in

Listen Labsif you're interviewing in more than one language. Its AI moderator works in 120+ languages and links every finding to its interviewMazeif you need people to use a prototype, not talk about it. Run an unmoderated test instead

Workflow: Interviews straight into the roadmap

  1. Run AI-moderated interviews at scale with Listen Labs
  2. Bring transcripts and feedback into Dovetail for cited themes
  3. Route ranked needs to Linear as issues

Result: Customer evidence linked to every roadmap issue. Open this workflow
