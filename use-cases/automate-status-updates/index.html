Automate status updates and stakeholder reports

Use the agent that lives where your work does. Notion Agent can run on a schedule to post updates and route tasks, and Claude can draft the part that needs judgment. Teams on Jira and Confluence should use Rovo, and research teams can let Dovetail's agents send recurring stakeholder updates.

Best for: PMs and leads who spend Friday afternoons writing updates.

Watch out: Automate the gathering, not the judgment. Read each update before it goes out, at least for the first month.

  1. Notion AgentPosts updates on a schedule
  2. ClaudeWrites what changed and why

How it works

  1. Notion Agent: Custom Agents run on a schedule to post updates, answer Slack questions and route tasks. Trial on Free; full agent on Business, $20/seat/mo
  2. Claude: Drafts the part that needs judgment: what changed, why it matters and what you need from people. Free plan; Pro $20/mo ($17 billed annually)

Or swap in

Rovoif your team runs on Jira and Confluence. Its agents work across bothDovetailif the updates are about research and customer insight
