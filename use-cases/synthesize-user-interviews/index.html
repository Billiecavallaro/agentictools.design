Synthesize user interviews into themes, answered

What's the best AI setup to synthesize user interviews into themes?

Bring the transcripts and the rest of your feedback into Dovetail, which turns them into insights that cite their sources. Then use Claude to shape the themes into a readout people will actually read, and log what matters in Linear.

  1. Dovetail: Pulls interviews and feedback from many sources into cited insights you can chat with.
  2. Claude: Turns the cited themes into a tight readout or brief for stakeholders.
  3. Linear Agent: Logs the needs that matter as issues, so the evidence travels with the work.

What does this setup cost?

Each tool's price, from its own website:

  • Dovetail: Paid; from $4,800/yr plus credits; 30-day free trial
  • Claude: Free plan; Pro $20/mo ($17 billed annually)
  • Linear Agent: Free plan includes agents; paid from $10/user/mo billed yearly

What can I use instead?

  • Outset, when: You haven't run the interviews yet. It writes the guide, runs them and delivers themes, highlight reels and decks.
  • Listen Labs, when: Your interviews happen in many languages. Every finding links back to the interview it came from.

Who is this setup for?
