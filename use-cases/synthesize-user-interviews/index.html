Synthesize user interviews into themes

Bring the transcripts and the rest of your feedback into Dovetail, which turns them into insights that cite their sources. Then use Claude to shape the themes into a readout people will actually read, and log what matters in Linear.

Best for: Researchers, designers and PMs sitting on a pile of transcripts.

Watch out: Read the quotes behind a theme before you present it. Citations are there so you can check the summary, so check it.

  1. DovetailFinds cited themes
  2. ClaudeWrites the readout
  3. Linear AgentLogs needs as issues

How it works

  1. Dovetail: Pulls interviews and feedback from many sources into cited insights you can chat with. Paid; from $4,800/yr plus credits; 30-day free trial
  2. Claude: Turns the cited themes into a tight readout or brief for stakeholders. Free plan; Pro $20/mo ($17 billed annually)
  3. Linear Agent: Logs the needs that matter as issues, so the evidence travels with the work. Free plan includes agents; paid from $10/user/mo billed yearly

Or swap in

Outsetif you haven't run the interviews yet. It writes the guide, runs them and delivers themes, highlight reels and decksListen Labsif your interviews happen in many languages. Every finding links back to the interview it came from

Workflow: Interviews straight into the roadmap

  1. Run AI-moderated interviews at scale with Listen Labs
  2. Bring transcripts and feedback into Dovetail for cited themes
  3. Route ranked needs to Linear as issues

Result: Customer evidence linked to every roadmap issue. Open this workflow
