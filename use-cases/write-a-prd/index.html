Write a PRD from meeting notes

Use ChatPRD. Paste in your meeting notes and it drafts the PRD, scores it, and can open it in v0, Lovable or Cursor when you're ready to build. Keep the final spec where your team works with Notion Agent.

Best for: Product managers who'd rather decide than format docs.

Watch out: Treat the score as a checklist, not a verdict. Only you know whether it's the right problem to solve.

  1. ChatPRDTurns notes into a PRD
  2. Notion AgentBuilds the spec page

How it works

  1. ChatPRD: Turns meeting notes into a PRD and scores it, so you see what's weak before the review. Free plan; Pro $15/mo
  2. Notion Agent: Builds and edits the spec page and its databases in your Notion workspace. Trial on Free; full agent on Business, $20/seat/mo

Or swap in

Claudeif you want a thinking partner for the hard tradeoffs, not a templateRovoif your specs live in Confluence and your work lives in Jira

Workflow: Idea to tested prototype in a day

  1. Draft the PRD from meeting notes in ChatPRD
  2. Open the spec in v0 to build a clickable app
  3. Run an unmoderated prototype test with Maze participants

Result: A working prototype with real user feedback attached. Open this workflow
