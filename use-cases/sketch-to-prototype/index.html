Turn a whiteboard sketch into a prototype

Photograph the sketch and let Stitch turn it into a digital design, paste that into Figma to clean it up, then make it clickable with Figma Make. If you're not a designer, Uizard is the gentler start.

Best for: Designers and PMs who think on paper first.

Watch out: Write short labels on the sketch, like search, filters or results. Clear words leave the generator less to guess.

  1. StitchTurns the photo into a design
  2. Figma MakeMakes it clickable

How it works

  1. Stitch: Turns a photo of a whiteboard sketch or a screenshot into a digital design you can paste into Figma, plus front-end code. See site
  2. Figma Make: Turns the cleaned-up frames into a working prototype. Click any element in the preview to restyle or re-prompt it. See site

Uizardif you're a PM or founder without design skills. It scans a hand-drawn wireframe into an editable mockupFigma agentif the screens should be built from your team's own Figma library from the start

Workflow: Whiteboard sketch to Figma prototype

  1. Photograph the sketch and generate screens in Stitch
  2. Paste the designs into Figma for cleanup
  3. Turn the frames into a working prototype with Figma Make
