Turn customer feedback into a spec

  1. Samepage BetsRanks feedback into bets
  2. ChatPRDWrites and scores the PRD
  3. Linear AgentTurns it into issues

Or swap in

Dovetailif you have a research teamRovoif you run on Jira

How it works

Samepage Bets pulls feedback from calls, tickets and chat into one place and ranks it into scored bets. Take the winning bet into ChatPRD to write and score the PRD, then hand the work out in Linear.

  1. Samepage Bets: Collects scattered feedback and ranks it into scored bets, then has agents research and spec the work, with you approving each step. Free plan; Pro $15/mo, Teams $25/user/mo billed yearly
  2. ChatPRD: Writes the PRD and scores it, so you know it's ready before engineers see it. Free plan; Pro $15/mo
  3. Linear Agent: Turns the spec into issues you can delegate to Cursor, Codex or ChatPRD, and shows each change and its reasoning. Free plan includes agents; paid from $10/user/mo billed yearly

Swap a tool in when it fits you better:

  • Dovetail: You're at a larger org with a research team. Its agents route ranked customer needs to Jira, Linear or Cursor.
  • Rovo: Your team runs on Jira and Confluence. Its agents draft roadmaps and route backlog work.

Watch out: Ranked feedback tells you what's loud, not what's important. Keep the call on which bet to take with a person.

Best for: PMs drowning in calls, tickets and Slack threads.

Workflow: Interviews straight into the roadmap

  1. Run AI-moderated interviews at scale with Listen Labs
  2. Bring transcripts and feedback into Dovetail for cited themes
  3. Route ranked needs to Linear as issues

Result: Customer evidence linked to every roadmap issue. Open this workflow
