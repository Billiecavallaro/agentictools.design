Turn customer feedback into a spec, answered

What's the best AI setup to turn customer feedback into a spec?

Samepage Bets pulls feedback from calls, tickets and chat into one place and ranks it into scored bets. Take the winning bet into ChatPRD to write and score the PRD, then hand the work out in Linear.

  1. Samepage Bets: Collects scattered feedback and ranks it into scored bets, then has agents research and spec the work, with you approving each step.
  2. ChatPRD: Writes the PRD and scores it, so you know it's ready before engineers see it.
  3. Linear Agent: Turns the spec into issues you can delegate to Cursor, Codex or ChatPRD, and shows each change and its reasoning.

What does this setup cost?

Each tool's price, from its own website:

  • Samepage Bets: Free plan; Pro $15/mo, Teams $25/user/mo billed yearly
  • ChatPRD: Free plan; Pro $15/mo
  • Linear Agent: Free plan includes agents; paid from $10/user/mo billed yearly

What can I use instead?

  • Dovetail, when: You're at a larger org with a research team. Its agents route ranked customer needs to Jira, Linear or Cursor.
  • Rovo, when: Your team runs on Jira and Confluence. Its agents draft roadmaps and route backlog work.

Who is this setup for?

PMs drowning in calls, tickets and Slack threads.

What should I watch out for?
