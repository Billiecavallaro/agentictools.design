Run a competitive analysis

How it works

Send ChatGPT agent to browse competitor sites and build the comparison, ask Claude to pull out the gaps and opportunities, and save the brief in Notion with Notion Agent. Check every claim against the sources before you share it.

  1. ChatGPT agent: Browses competitor sites in its own browser and builds the comparison as a spreadsheet or slides, asking before important steps. Included with ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise
  2. Claude: Reads the comparison and writes up the gaps and opportunities. Free plan; Pro $20/mo ($17 billed annually)
  3. Notion Agent: Saves the brief and next steps where the team will find them. Trial on Free; full agent on Business, $20/seat/mo

Swap a tool in when it fits you better:

  • Comet: You'd rather browse yourself. Its assistant compares how different sources cover a topic without leaving the page.

Watch out: Agents read marketing pages, and marketing pages flatter. Pair the scan with real reviews or customer calls before you draw conclusions.

Best for: PMs and designers prepping a strategy review or a kickoff.

Workflow: Competitive scan to product brief
