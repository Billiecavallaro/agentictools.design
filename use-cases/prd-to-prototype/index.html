What's the best AI setup to turn a PRD into a clickable prototype?

Draft or tighten the PRD in ChatPRD, open it in v0 to build a working web prototype, then put it in front of real users with a Maze test. You end up with a clickable app and real feedback on it, often in a single day.

Best for: PMs and designers with a spec who need something people can click

The setup, in order

ChatPRD → v0 → Maze
  1. ChChatPRDChatPRD · Product & specs

    Turns your notes into a PRD and scores it, so weak spots show up before you build. It can open the spec straight in v0.

    Free plan; Pro $15/mo
  2. v0v0Vercel · Prototyping

    Builds the clickable web app from the spec. Tweak the UI visually in Design Mode, then deploy in one click and share the link.

    Free plan (7 messages/day); Plus $30/user/mo
  3. MaMazeMaze · Research

    Runs an unmoderated test of the prototype with people from its participant panel and writes the report for you.

    Free trial; plans, see site
