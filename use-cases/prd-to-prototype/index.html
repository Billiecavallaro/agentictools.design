Turn a PRD into a clickable prototype

  1. ChatPRDWrites and scores the PRD
  2. v0Builds the clickable app
  3. MazeTests it with real users

Or swap in

Magic Patternsif it must look like your productLovableif you need a real appFigma Makeif your team lives in Figma

How it works

Draft or tighten the PRD in ChatPRD, open it in v0 to build a working web prototype, then put it in front of real users with a Maze test. You end up with a clickable app and real feedback on it, often in a single day.

  1. ChatPRD: Turns your notes into a PRD and scores it, so weak spots show up before you build. It can open the spec straight in v0. Free plan; Pro $15/mo
  2. v0: Builds the clickable web app from the spec. Tweak the UI visually in Design Mode, then deploy in one click and share the link. Free plan (7 messages/day); Plus $30/user/mo
  3. Maze: Runs an unmoderated test of the prototype with people from its participant panel and writes the report for you. Free trial; plans, see site

Swap a tool in when it fits you better:

  • Magic Patterns: The prototype has to look like your real product. Import your component library and it turns the PRD into high-fidelity UI.
  • Lovable: You need more than a demo. Hosting, sign-in and payments come built in.
  • Figma Make: Your team lives in Figma and wants the prototype next to the designs.

Watch out: Don't polish the prototype before the test. The point is to find out which parts of the spec are wrong while they're still cheap to change.

Best for: PMs and designers with a spec who need something people can click.

Workflow: Idea to tested prototype in a day

  1. Draft the PRD from meeting notes in ChatPRD
  2. Open the spec in v0 to build a clickable app
  3. Run an unmoderated prototype test with Maze participants
